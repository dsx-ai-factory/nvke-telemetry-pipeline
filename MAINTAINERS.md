# Maintainers

NKTP is maintained by the `@dsx-ai-factory/nktp-maintainers` team referenced from
[.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). That team is the authoritative,
always-current membership list; this file describes what maintainers do and how
to reach them rather than duplicating it.

## Contacting the maintainers

- **Reviews and technical questions:** mention `@dsx-ai-factory/nktp-maintainers` on
  the relevant issue or pull request.
- **Bugs, proposals, and questions:** use the issue forms described under
  [Reporting bugs, proposing changes, and asking questions](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-bugs-proposing-changes-and-asking-questions).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not use GitHub. Follow the process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of Conduct concerns:** GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com, per
  [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What maintainers do

CODEOWNERS assigns the whole tree to one team rather than splitting it by
directory, so maintainers hold responsibility for the project as a whole:

- Review and merge pull requests under the requirements in
  [Review process](CONTRIBUTING.md#review-process).
- Triage incoming issues.
- Decide what the chart installs by default, and which bundled dependencies in
  `nktp/Chart.yaml` are added, removed, or upgraded.
- Version, tag, and publish chart releases as described in
  [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md).
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainership currently sits with the NVIDIA team that develops NKTP. If you
have been contributing and want a larger role, open an issue and start the
conversation there.
